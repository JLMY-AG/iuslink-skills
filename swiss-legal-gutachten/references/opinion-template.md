# Gutachten output template

Adapt the headings and depth to the mandate. Omit sections that genuinely do not apply; never fill them with boilerplate.

```markdown
# [Title / legal question]

**Relevant date:** [date/current law as retrieved]
**Jurisdiction:** [federal/canton(s)]
**Research status:** [complete for stated scope / qualified / material gap]

## Executive summary

- **Short answer:** [direct answer]
- **Principal reason:** [decisive verified rule and application]
- **Material qualification:** [fact, counter-authority, discretion, or source gap]
- **Overall confidence:** established / fact-dependent / unresolved — [reason]

## Mandate, questions, and scope

### Questions
1. [precise question]

### Scope and exclusions
- [jurisdiction, date, excluded topics, source limits]

## Facts and assumptions

### Supplied facts
- [fact and, where available, record reference]

### Disputed or open facts
- [fact and effect on the analysis]

### Assumptions
- [assumption and why it is needed]

## Legal assessment

### 1. [Issue]

**Provisional answer:** [one or two sentences]

#### Governing law
- [verified provision/version or decision/consideration]

#### Analysis and application
[Separate what the source establishes from how it applies to the facts. Address each material legal requirement.]

#### Counterarguments and uncertainty
[Strongest plausible counter-reading, limiting authority, disputed fact, or source gap.]

#### Issue conclusion
[Result.] **Confidence:** established / fact-dependent / unresolved — [reason].

## Overall conclusion

[Answer the mandate without becoming broader or more certain than the issue conclusions.]

## Recommendations

1. [Action tied to a finding, missing fact, or identified legal risk.]

## Research limitations

- [Unretrievable historical version, cantonal metadata-only source, unpublished practice, unsuccessful search scope, or other material limit.]

## Primary sources

### Legislation
- [Enactment, systematic number, article, applicable version/date, language, official identifier]

### Case law
- [Court, citation/docket, date, relevant consideration, iuslink signature/identifier]

### Cantonal source links
- [Canton, systematic number, title, official URL; state whether the text itself was verified]
```

## Drafting rules

- Quote only passages actually retrieved and needed for the reasoning.
- Prefer precise paraphrase with a pinpoint citation over long block quotes.
- Repeat a source only when needed for readability; one complete source-register entry is enough.
- Recommendations must follow from the analysis, not introduce new unsupported legal claims.
